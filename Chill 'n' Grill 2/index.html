About The Chill Grill

Welcome to Chill and Grill, the famous smoke yard where we sell amazing foods and drinks! Our passion is taking you on a tasty route through flavour town with every bite.

From perfectly smoked chicken meals to our signature pulled pork, every dish is crafted with love and attention to detail. Our alcohol drinks perfectly complement our smoky creations.

What sets us apart? Our incredible staff will make sure you have a nice stay, always checking to see if you need help, any form of request, or direction. We're here to embark you on this incredible culinary journey!
